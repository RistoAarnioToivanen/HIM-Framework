# HIM Manifesto

## Making the Invisible Visible

Every day, millions of people interact with digital systems.

Some succeed effortlessly.

Others struggle.

Organizations invest enormous resources in technology, yet many decisions are still based on assumptions rather than evidence.

When a problem occurs, the first question is often:

> *"What is wrong with the software?"*

The Human Interaction Measurement (HIM) Framework asks a different question:

> **"Why did the interaction fail?"**

The answer is not always found in the software.

It may be found in the user interface.

It may be found in training.

It may be found in documentation.

It may be found in business processes.

It may be found in organizational structures.

It may be found in communication.

Or it may be found in several factors working together.

HIM does not seek someone to blame.

HIM seeks to understand.

Understanding creates knowledge.

Knowledge enables improvement.

Improvement creates better digital environments.

The purpose of HIM is simple:

> **To make the invisible visible.**

---

## Our Principles

We believe that:

- Evidence is more valuable than assumptions.
- Understanding comes before improvement.
- Technology exists to support people.
- Human Interaction can be observed, measured and continuously improved.
- Better decisions are made when they are based on objective evidence.

---

## Our Vision

We envision a future where organizations no longer guess why people struggle.

Instead, they understand.

Digital systems should not only function correctly.

They should help people succeed.

---

## Our Mission

To establish Human Interaction Measurement as an internationally recognized framework for understanding, measuring and improving Human Interaction within digital environments.

---

## Our Commitment

The HIM Framework is openly published.

Everyone is invited to study it, challenge it, improve it and contribute to its evolution.

The future of Human Interaction should be built on evidence, collaboration and continuous learning.

---

> **We do not guess.**
>
> **We measure.**
>
> **We understand.**
>
> **We improve.**
>
> **We make the invisible visible.**
