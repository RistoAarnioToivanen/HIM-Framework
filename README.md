# Why HIM?

Organizations invest billions in digital technologies.

Software quality is traditionally evaluated through technical characteristics such as:

- Performance
- Reliability
- Security
- Functionality

These characteristics are essential.

However, they do not explain why people succeed or fail when interacting with digital systems.

The Human Interaction Measurement (HIM) Framework introduces another equally important perspective:

> **How well does the entire digital environment enable people to succeed?**

HIM does not begin by asking:

> **"Where is the fault?"**

Instead, HIM asks:

> **"What prevents people from succeeding?"**

The answer may be found in:

- Software
- User Experience
- Business Processes
- Training
- Documentation
- Communication
- Organizational Structures
- Leadership
- System Configuration
- Or any combination of these factors.

HIM is designed to identify **root causes**, not assumptions.

> **Evidence before assumptions.**

---

# Vision

Digital systems should do more than function correctly.

They should enable people to work safely, efficiently, confidently and successfully.

The HIM Framework aims to become an internationally recognized reference framework for understanding and continuously improving Human Interaction across digital environments.

---

# Mission

To establish a common language, methodology and internationally recognized framework for observing, measuring, understanding and improving Human Interaction.

---

# Core Principles

The HIM Framework is based on the following principles:

- Human Experience
- Human-Centered Design
- Evidence-Based Assessment
- Root Cause Analysis
- Cognitive Load
- Workflow Efficiency
- Information Quality
- Decision Support
- Organizational Friction
- Continuous Improvement

---

# Open Framework

The HIM Framework is openly published to encourage international collaboration.

Researchers, organizations, software vendors, public institutions and professionals are invited to study, discuss and contribute to the continuous development of the framework.

---

# Long-Term Goal

To establish the Human Interaction Measurement (HIM) Framework as an internationally recognized reference model for understanding, evaluating and improving Human Interaction within digital environments.
